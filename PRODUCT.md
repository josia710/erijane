# PRODUCT.md

## Register
**brand** — marketing / fitness content site. Design IS the product surface.

## Purpose
Local Laravel + Livewire study recreation of [chloeting.com](https://chloeting.com/). Match the official site’s UI/UX for learning. Not affiliated with the Chloe Ting brand.

## Users
Developers studying a multi-page fitness marketing IA (programs, videos, recipes, store, community, auth).

## Brand personality
Clean, athletic, approachable, light, confident.

## Anti-references
- Invented cyan/Barlow “fitness” palettes
- AI purple gradients
- Inter as default (live site uses Manrope + Poppins)
- Real checkout or copyrighted video streams in this clone

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast for body text; visible focus; `prefers-reduced-motion` for any motion.

## Constraints
- Keep footer study disclaimer
- Cart/video: exact UI shells only (no real commerce/streams)
- Preserve Laravel + Livewire structure; restyle views/CSS
